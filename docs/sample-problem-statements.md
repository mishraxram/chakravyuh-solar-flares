# SAMPLE problem statements (invented for practice, NOT the real ones)

Use these for the dress rehearsal on 14 Oct. Pick one, paste it into /ps-decode.

1. AI Solutions (sample): Rural health workers write patient notes by hand in Hindi and many notes get lost. Build a tool that turns a short typed or spoken symptom note into a structured, readable summary and flags urgent cases.
2. Sustainable Development (sample): A college campus wastes electricity because lights and fans stay on in empty rooms. Build a simple system that shows where energy is wasted and nudges students and staff to fix it.
3. AI-Based Cybersecurity (sample): Students lose money to phishing links and fake scholarship messages on WhatsApp. Build a checker that takes a message or link and explains in simple Hindi or English why it looks risky.
4. Security and Safety (sample): Late-night travel around Lucknow feels unsafe for students. Build a lightweight tool that helps a user share a live trip with trusted contacts and raises an alert if the trip goes off track.
